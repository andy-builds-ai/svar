# Pellwick Joinery — From Enquiry to Fitting

*How a job runs through our workshop, step by step. Written for customers,
and used by the team as the reference for how we work.*

## Overview

| Step | Who | Typical time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Enquiry | Anja | reply within 2 working days |
| 2. Survey | Martin | within 3 weeks of the enquiry |
| 3. Quote | Martin | within 10 working days of the survey |
| 4. Order and deposit | you, Anja | quote valid 60 days |
| 5. Drawings and approval | Martin, you | within 2 weeks of the deposit |
| 6. Making | workshop | 6 to 10 weeks |
| 7. Fitting | Rory and one joiner | 1 day per 2–3 windows |
| 8. Handover and aftercare | Rory, Anja | on the fitting day, check visit within a year |

From deposit to fitting this usually adds up to **8 to 12 weeks**, as
written on the quote.

## 1. Enquiry

Send an email to workshop@pellwick.example or call the office. Tell us:

- what you would like made or repaired,
- your address,
- whether the house is listed or in a conservation area,
- a few photographs, if you can.

Anja replies within 2 working days and offers survey dates.

**Listed buildings and conservation areas.** Replacing windows in a listed
building needs consent from the local planning authority. Getting the
consent is your responsibility, but we provide the drawings and the
specification the authority asks for, free of charge when you order.

## 2. Survey

Martin visits, usually for 45 to 90 minutes. He measures every opening,
checks the condition of the existing frames and the wall around them, and
talks through timber, glazing, colour and hardware. For staircases he also
measures floor-to-floor height and the position of walls.

The survey costs **£60**, deducted from the final invoice if you order (see
*Quotes, Prices and Payment*).

## 3. Quote

The written quote arrives within 10 working days. It is itemised and shows
the expected lead time. It is valid for 60 days.

## 4. Order and deposit

You accept the quote by email. When the **30 % deposit** has arrived, Anja
puts the job into the workshop schedule and confirms an expected fitting
week.

## 5. Drawings and approval

For windows, doors and staircases, Martin draws each item: sizes, sections,
glazing bars, opening direction and hardware. You receive the drawings by
email and approve them in writing.

**Nothing is cut until the drawings are approved.** Changes until then are
free. The time until approval counts toward the lead time, so a quick reply
keeps the fitting date.

## 6. Making

Each job has one lead joiner: Dev for windows and doors, Tomasz for
staircases, Imogen for fitted furniture. Apprentices work with them.

What happens in the workshop:

1. Timber is selected and left to acclimatise for at least a week.
2. Parts are machined and the joints cut.
3. Frames are assembled and glued; staircases are dry-assembled to check the
   fit.
4. Glass is ordered to measured sizes once the frames are made.
5. Windows and doors are primed, undercoated and top coated in the workshop.
   Oak is oiled.
6. Glazing and hardware are fitted.

When everything is finished, Anja sends the invoice for the **60 % stage
payment** and books the fitting date once it has arrived. You are welcome to
visit the workshop and see your windows before they leave; please arrange
it with Anja.

## 7. Fitting

**Before we come:**

- clear about one metre inside each window or door,
- take down curtains, blinds and poles,
- tell us about alarm sensors on windows,
- make sure there is parking for a van close to the house.

**On the day:**

- Rory and one joiner arrive at about 08:00.
- Old windows are removed one at a time; each opening is closed again the
  same day. We never leave a house open overnight.
- New frames are fixed, sealed outside and finished inside.
- Small making good around the frame is included; larger plaster repairs
  are not, and are shown on the quote if we expect them.
- Old windows and waste are taken away.

Two or three windows per day is typical. A front door takes most of a day;
a staircase takes two to four days.

If we find hidden damage — for example a rotten lintel — we stop, show you,
and agree what to do before continuing.

## 8. Handover and aftercare

Before leaving, Rory:

- opens and closes every window and door with you,
- shows how locks, stays and sashes work,
- hands over keys, spare paint and the leaflet *Window and Door Care and Our
  Guarantee*,
- issues the certificate for the replacement windows, which is registered
  through our competent person scheme within 30 days.

The **10 % balance** is due within 14 days after fitting. The guarantee
starts on the fitting day and applies once the invoice is paid.

Within the first year you can book a free check visit, when Rory adjusts
hinges and locks and looks at the finish.

## If something goes wrong

Tell us straight away, by email with photographs. The steps and response
times for guarantee claims are in *Window and Door Care and Our Guarantee*.
If you are not happy with how we handled something, write to Martin
Pellwick directly at workshop@pellwick.example, marked "for Martin"; he
replies personally within 5 working days.
