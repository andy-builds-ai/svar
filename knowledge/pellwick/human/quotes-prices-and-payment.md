# Pellwick Joinery — Quotes, Prices and Payment

*Applies to quotes issued from 1 April 2026. All prices include VAT.*

## Survey visit

Every job starts with a survey at your house. Martin Pellwick measures,
looks at the condition of what is there and talks through options.

- **Survey fee:** £60.
- The fee is **deducted from the final invoice** if you place an order
  within the validity of the quote.
- Surveys are booked by Anja in the office, usually within three weeks of
  your enquiry.

We do not quote from photographs alone, except for fitted furniture where
you can send exact measurements; then the survey fee does not apply until
we visit to confirm.

## Quotes

After the survey you receive a written, itemised quote **within 10 working
days**. The quote shows:

- each item (for example "box sash window, bedroom 2, larch, painted white"),
- materials and glazing,
- making and fitting,
- removal and disposal of old windows or doors,
- any making good (small plaster or paint repairs around the new frame),
- the expected lead time.

A quote is **valid for 60 days**. Timber prices change during the year;
after 60 days we check the prices again before accepting an order.

of **£48**, plus materials. We always ask before doing extra work.

## Deposit and payment

| Stage | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | **30 %** of the quote | When you accept the quote. Your job enters the workshop schedule once the deposit has arrived. |
| Before fitting | **60 %** | When the joinery is finished in the workshop, before we book the fitting date. |
| Balance | **10 %** | Within **14 days** after fitting is complete. |

For jobs under £1,000, such as a single repair, we ask for full payment
within 14 days after the work instead of a deposit.

We accept bank transfer and card payments by phone. We do not accept cash
for amounts over £500.

Invoices are sent by email from workshop@pellwick.example. The bank details
on invoices never change; if you ever receive an email saying our bank
details have changed, please call the office before paying.

## Changes after ordering

- Changes before we cut timber — usually within the first two weeks after
  the deposit — are free, apart from any price difference.
- Changes after we have started making are charged at the hourly rate plus
  materials already used.
- Glass is ordered to size. Changing a glazing choice after the glass order
  costs the price of the glass.

## Cancellation

You can cancel within **14 days** of accepting the quote and receive your
deposit back in full, as long as we have not yet started making at your
request. After that, we keep the part of the deposit that covers timber and
work already done, and refund the rest.

## Delays

Our usual lead time is **8 to 12 weeks** from deposit to fitting. The
expected lead time is written on your quote. If we fall more than four weeks
behind the date on the quote, we tell you why and you may cancel with a full
refund of the deposit.

Delays caused on site — for example if the openings are not ready because
other trades have not finished — are rebooked for the next free fitting
slot.

## Guarantee

Guarantee terms are described in *Window and Door Care and Our Guarantee*.
The guarantee starts on the day fitting is complete and applies once the
invoice is paid in full.
