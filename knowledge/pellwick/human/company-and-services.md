# Pellwick Joinery — About Us and Our Services

*Fictional company, written for the Andur demo.*

## A family workshop

Pellwick Joinery is a small joinery workshop that makes and fits timber
windows, doors, staircases and fitted furniture for houses, mostly within
about 40 miles of the workshop. Harold Pellwick founded the business in 1998
in a rented barn with one bench saw and one apprentice. His son Martin
Pellwick trained in the workshop, and has run the business since 2017.

## What we make

### Timber windows

Our main work. We make **flush casement windows** and **box sash windows**,
both as new windows and as exact copies of old ones for period houses. All
windows are double glazed as standard; slim heritage double glazing is
available for listed buildings where the planning authority allows it.

### External doors

Front and back doors, stable doors and French doors, with frames. We fit
multi-point locks as standard.

### Staircases

Straight, winding and open-tread staircases in oak or pine, and repairs to
old staircases: new treads, handrails and spindles. Every new staircase is
measured on site and dry-assembled in the workshop before fitting.

### Fitted furniture

Alcove cupboards, bookshelves, window seats, understairs storage and
built-in wardrobes. We do not make fitted kitchens.

### Repairs and restoration

Sash window overhauls (new cords, beads and draught strips), splicing in new
timber where sills or bottom rails have rotted, and repairs to old doors.
Often a good repair saves an original window; we will say so when it does.

## What we do not do

- Plastic or aluminium windows.
- Roofing, structural carpentry or building extensions.
- Painting of whole rooms. We paint and finish what we make.
- Work further than about 40 miles away, except for staircases.

## Timber we use

| Timber | Used for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| European oak | Staircases, doors, sills, furniture | Hard-wearing, beautiful when oiled |
| Siberian larch | Windows and doors to be painted | Stable, naturally durable, sustainably sourced |
| Engineered pine | Painted furniture, internal joinery | Straight and stable, good value |

All timber comes from suppliers with certified sustainable sourcing. We keep
the certificates in the office and give copies on request.

## How we work

1. **Enquiry and survey** — we visit, measure and talk through options.
2. **Quote** — a written, itemised quote within 10 working days of the
   survey.
3. **Order and deposit** — the job enters our workshop schedule.
4. **Making** — in our workshop, typically 8 to 12 weeks after the order.
5. **Fitting** — by our own fitters, never by subcontractors.
6. **Aftercare** — a check visit on request within the first year.

The details, including how long each step takes, are in *From Enquiry to
Fitting*. Prices, deposits and payment are in *Quotes, Prices and
Payment*. Guarantees and maintenance are in *Window and Door Care and Our
Guarantee*.

## Training

We have trained at least one apprentice every year since 2001. Apprentices
spend four days a week in the workshop and one day at college. Several of
our former apprentices now run their own workshops.

## Insurance and qualifications

We carry public liability insurance and employer's liability insurance;
copies are available on request. Our window fitters are registered with the
local competent person scheme for replacement windows, so we can certify
the work ourselves and you do not need a separate building control
application for like-for-like window replacement.
